## Brand & Style

This design system expresses a fusion of modern editorial minimalism and soft chic glam, built specifically for an aspirational, design-conscious audience seeking high-performance beauty rituals. The tone evokes understated opulence, tactile intimacy, and architectural poise—reminiscent of high-end Parisian ateliers meeting contemporary digital flagship boutiques.

Key emotional anchors:
- **Luminous Calm:** Airy spatial compositions, gentle contrasts, and whisper-quiet transitions elevate product storytelling without visual noise.
- **Warm Tactility:** Surfaces mirror fine cosmetic compacts, satin formulas, and unlacquered packaging rather than sterile tech interfaces.
- **Editorial Precision:** Typographic pairings command authority like a luxury beauty monograph, balancing grand titles with crisp, high-utility product data.

Visual direction:
- **Clean Modern Minimalist + Soft Glam:** Generous, intentional whitespace punctuated by warm blush tones, soft rose bronze accents, and razor-sharp typographic geometry.
- **Quiet Surfaces:** Deep-layered porcelain backdrops paired with immaculate pure-white product decks and gossamer-fine borders.

## Colors

The palette reproduces the tactile nuance of cosmetic formulations—powders, creams, and polished metals—balanced against luminous canvas tones to keep beauty imagery vibrant and true to life.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#C88A75` - Warm Rose Bronze):** Anchors focal actions, active states, key toggles, and primary CTA accents. Deepens to `#B5735D` on interaction.
- **Secondary (`#F3E8E2` - Champagne Blush):** Acts as a structural tint for badge chips, promo banners, hover washes, and secondary button fills.
- **Tertiary (`#B5735D` - Deep Terracotta Bronze):** Reserved for high-emphasis editorial labels, interactive active highlights, and critical CTA hover triggers.
- **Neutral Primary (`#1A1615` - Deep Espresso Charcoal):** Grounded typographic anchor. Used for principal headlines, prices, primary icons, and prominent solid buttons.
- **Neutral Secondary (`#2D2726` - Soft Charcoal):** Secondary typography, body text, and navigational elements.
- **Muted Neutral (`#7A706B` - Smoked Taupe):** Tertiary captions, metadata, stock levels, and disabled indicators.
- **Surface Canvas (`#FAF8F5`):** Ambient background tone providing organic warmth over clinical white.
- **Surface Layer 2 (`#F5F1EB`):** Container tone for inset modules, drawers, and secondary sidebars.
- **Surface Pure (`#FFFFFF`):** High-impact card foreground, flyouts, and modal panels.
- **Border Subtle (`#EAE4DC`):** Hairline structure across panels, tables, inputs, and card perimeters.
- **Status Accents:**
  - *Success:* `#4D7C5F` (Muted Sage)
  - *Warning:* `#C28B38` (Antique Amber)
  - *Critical:* `#A84343` (Cinnabar)

## Typography

The typography pairs the high-fashion editorial gravitas of **Playfair Display** with the sharp, structural legibility of **Plus Jakarta Sans**.

### Usage Guidelines
- **Headlines & Editorial Callouts:** Use Playfair Display for dramatic narrative moments, collection names, hero features, and section headers. Keep tracking slightly compressed on display sizes (`-0.01em` to `-0.02em`) to mirror luxury print magazines.
- **Interface & Operational Typography:** Reserve Plus Jakarta Sans strictly for body descriptions, checkout steps, interactive menus, form inputs, SKU attributes, and financial metrics.
- **Uppercase Badges & Category Trackers:** Render `label-caps` in clean uppercase with `0.12em` letter spacing to ground delicate product badges, ingredient highlights, and metadata tags without dominating the composition.
- **Numbers & Monetary Values:** Format currency values exclusively in Plus Jakarta Sans SemiBold/Bold for immediate, crystal-clear pricing comprehension across responsive product cards and invoice tables.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is grounded in a responsive 12-column grid system paired with airy rhythmic pacing that allows cosmetics imagery to breathe.

### Grid Rhythm & Viewports
- **Desktop (1280px+):** 12-column fluid grid, max-width `1440px`, with `margin: 3rem` (48px) and `gutter: 1.5rem` (24px).
- **Tablet (768px – 1279px):** 8-column grid with `margin-tablet: 2rem` (32px) and `gutter: 1.25rem` (20px).
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column grid with `margin-mobile: 1.25rem` (20px) and `gutter-sm: 1rem` (16px).

### Spacing Philosophy
- Use `space-2xl` for sweeping whitespace between major editorial collection blocks and interactive storefront displays.
- Maintain `space-lg` to `space-xl` as internal canvas padding within card decks and drawer containers.
- Enforce strict micro-spacing (`space-xs` and `space-sm`) for compact cosmetic shade swatches, pill tags, and form label/input pairs to preserve precise typographic alignment.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses ethereal, diffuse shadow casting paired with crisp hairline borders, deliberately avoiding heavy corporate drop shadows. Surfaces mimic pristine acrylic compacts, translucent frosted glass, and soft ambient room lighting.

### Elevation Levels
- **Level 0 (Flat / Canvas):** Default `#FAF8F5` canvas and `#F5F1EB` section groupings. No shadow.
- **Level 1 (Card & Shelf Resting):** Pure white `#FFFFFF` surfaces bounded by a `1px solid #EAE4DC` border, accompanied by a subtle ambient glow: `0px 4px 20px -2px rgba(26, 22, 21, 0.03), 0px 1px 4px 0px rgba(200, 138, 117, 0.04)`.
- **Level 2 (Hover & Active Product):** Slight upward visual lift on card hover: `0px 12px 32px -4px rgba(26, 22, 21, 0.06), 0px 4px 12px 0px rgba(200, 138, 117, 0.08)`. Border shifts dynamically to `#D8CDC2`.
- **Level 3 (Flyouts, Menus & Drawers):** Cart drawer, mega menu dropdowns, and context toolbars: `0px 20px 48px -8px rgba(26, 22, 21, 0.08), 0px 2px 8px 0px rgba(26, 22, 21, 0.03)`.
- **Level 4 (Modals & Quick View):** Central overlays: `0px 32px 72px -12px rgba(26, 22, 21, 0.16)`. Accompanied by a tinted backdrop blur overlay: `rgba(26, 22, 21, 0.35)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(8px)`.
- **Frosted Elements (Navigation Bar / Floating Bar):** Glassmorphic base `rgba(250, 248, 245, 0.85)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` and a subtle baseline border `1px solid rgba(234, 228, 220, 0.7)`.

## Shapes

The design system incorporates geometric elegance (`roundedness: 2` = 0.5rem base) to echo the refined, sculptured edges of luxury cosmetic packaging. 

### Component Rounding Rules
- **Base UI Elements (Buttons, Inputs, Selects):** `rounded-md` (0.5rem / 8px). Delivers a tailored architectural edge that balances softness with structure.
- **Cards, Drawers & Content Containers:** `rounded-xl` (1.5rem / 24px). Creates an inviting contour around product photography, reviews, and admin metric widgets.
- **Pills & Badges:** `rounded-full` (9999px). Reserved exclusively for status indicators, formula attributes (e.g., *Vegan*, *Hydrating*), cart counters, and color swatch selector frames.
- **Nested Inner Modules:** Any surface nested inside a `rounded-xl` container must use `rounded-lg` (1rem / 16px) or `rounded-md` (0.5rem / 8px) to maintain harmonious concentric curvature.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Controls
- **Primary Luxe Button:** Solid deep espresso background (`#1A1615`), crisp white typography (`#FFFFFF`), `rounded-md` (8px), height `48px` (large) or `40px` (medium). On hover, transitions to warm bronze tint `#2D2726` with subtle translateY(-1px).
- **Secondary Champagne Button:** Soft warm champagne blush background (`#F3E8E2`), primary dark label (`#1A1615`), border `1px solid #E2D3CA`. On hover, surface lightens subtly with an enriched rose bronze outline (`#C88A75`).
- **Ghost / Outlined Button:** Transparent background, `1px solid #EAE4DC`, `#1A1615` text. On hover: background shifts to `#FAF8F5` with `#C88A75` border.
- **Accent Bronze Button:** Reserved for "Add to Bag" high-intent actions. Background `#C88A75`, white typography, smooth transition to `#B5735D` on active/press.

### Product Cards (`rounded-xl`)
- Pure white background (`#FFFFFF`) with `1px solid #EAE4DC`.
- Image aspect ratio locked at `4:5` (editorial portrait), set against a soft off-white canvas backdrop (`#FAF8F5`).
- Subtle floating badge in top-left corner (e.g., *Bestseller*, *Clean Formula*).
- Interactive swatch row positioned directly beneath the title showing circular shade pellets (`20px x 20px`) with a floating hover ring.
- Fixed price display in `price-md` (`Plus Jakarta Sans Bold`) next to inline rating star counters in soft bronze `#C88A75`.
- Floating quick-add icon button in bottom right, transitioning on hover from subtle opacity to full high-contrast presence.

### Badges & Pill Tags
- **Luxe Badges:** Height `24px`, padding `0 10px`, `rounded-full`.
- **Rose Gold Accent Badge:** Background `#F3E8E2`, text `#8F4F3B`, border `1px solid rgba(200, 138, 117, 0.3)`. Subtly glowing with a soft drop shadow: `0px 2px 8px rgba(200, 138, 117, 0.15)`.
- **Neutral Tag:** Background `#F5F1EB`, text `#2D2726`, border `1px solid #EAE4DC`.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Text Inputs:** Height `48px`, background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #EAE4DC`, `rounded-md`, typography `body-md`. Focus state introduces a refined 1.5px ring in `#C88A75` with `0 0 0 3px rgba(200, 138, 117, 0.12)`.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** `18px x 18px` square (`rounded: 4px` for check, circle for radio). Unchecked: `1.5px solid #D8CDC2`. Checked: filled `#1A1615` with pure white indicator checkmark or bronze dot.

### Lists & Tables (Admin & Customer Portal)
- Table headers set in `label-caps` (`#7A706B`), bottom border `1px solid #EAE4DC`, height `44px`.
- Table rows: alternating hover state `#FAF8F5`, row height `64px`, bottom divider `1px solid #F5F1EB`.
- Metric KPI cards for Admin portal: `rounded-xl`, `#FFFFFF` base, subtle `#EAE4DC` border, pairing large numerical figures in Plus Jakarta Sans with trend indicators in Sage (`#4D7C5F`) or Bronze (`#C88A75`).

### Cosmetics-Specific Components
- **Shade Finder Swatch Selector:** Micro-grid of circular swatches with active status indicated by a `2px` offset outline ring in `#C88A75` with `2px` white buffer.
- **Ingredient Accordion:** Minimalist row divider with delicate `+` / `−` iconography in `#1A1615`. Smooth height transition revealing clean `body-md` typography.
- **Bag / Cart Drawer:** Floating right panel (`max-width: 440px`), pure white surface, top free-shipping progress tracker highlighted in warm rose bronze `#C88A75`, and sticky checkout footer with calculated subtotal in `price-lg`.
